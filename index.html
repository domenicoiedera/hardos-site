Three steps, one record

The bot is the easy part. The reason a business can say yes is what happens around it.

01

Point it at the work

Onboarding packs, KYC files, research, drafting. A repeatable job your team already runs by hand.

02

Set the limits

Who can start it, what it may touch, what it must never do. Limits are part of the job, not an afterthought.

03

Read the record

Every run leaves an account of itself. Review the output, keep the evidence, answer the client who asks.

Built for the questions your client will ask

Not a feature list. The four questions that decide whether AI is allowed near professional work.

  • Who approved this output? A named person, at a named step, not an implicit yes.
  • What did it touch? The files and data behind the run, listed.
  • Where did the data go? Stated plainly, before the first job runs, not in an appendix.
  • What happens when it is wrong? The output is a draft with a reviewer, not a verdict.
